# Code of conduct

Out of Tokens exists so that people who ran out of budget can still put their ideas on the
record. Many of them are sharing something they cared about and could not finish. Treat
every submission with that in mind.

## Expected

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests and reviews. Criticise the idea or the code, and
  leave the person out of it.
- Credit people for their ideas and their work.
- Assume good faith about cost and circumstances. Nobody has to justify why they could not
  pay for something.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or remarks about a person's identity, country, language, income or
  background.
- Submitting someone else's idea as your own, or changing the authors of an existing idea.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Spam, advertising, and ideas whose purpose is to harm people or break the law.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the repository owner, through the contact listed on their
GitHub profile, or with GitHub's "Report content" link on the comment itself. Reports are
kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close issues, and block accounts that break these
rules. A person who disagrees with a decision can ask a second maintainer to review it.

This text is short on purpose. Where it is silent, the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the guide.
